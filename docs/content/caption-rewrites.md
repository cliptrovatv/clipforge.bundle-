# ClipTrovaTV — title & caption rewrites

Based on the public view counts of the latest 30 YouTube Shorts, 24 Instagram Reels
and 22 TikToks (checked 2026-11-24). Your clips that got 100+ views name **who** did **what**
in the first words. The ones that stalled were vague ("look Baby Abandon"), were compilations,
or were posted minutes after another clip.

## The formula

**`[Streamer] [does one specific thing] in GTA 6 [emoji]`** — at most ~60 characters, so
it isn't cut off on any platform.

- Put the streamer's name first. People search for streamers by name.
- One moment per clip. "Day 3 best moments" got 2 views; single moments got 100–370.
- Credit line in every caption: `🎥 @streamer (live on Twitch/Kick/YouTube)`. Add
  whatever tags and links your campaign rules require.
- Use 3–5 hashtags at most: `#GTA6 #StreamerName #GTA6Clips` plus one campaign tag if required.
- Leave 60+ minutes between posts on the same account, and never repost the same clip
  to the same account.

### Caption template (works on all three platforms)

```
[Title line]
[One line of context or a question: "Would you have restarted?"]
🎥 @streamer — live on [platform]
#GTA6 #StreamerName #GTA6Clips
```
